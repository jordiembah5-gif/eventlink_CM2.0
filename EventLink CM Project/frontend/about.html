Our story

Bringing Cameroon's events
to one place

EventLink CM was born out of a simple problem: too many great events were happening across Cameroon, but too few people knew about them.

Our Goal

Make event discovery simple, free, and accessible for every person in Cameroon — from Yaoundé to Buea to Bamenda.

Our Vision

A connected, thriving event community where organisers and attendees can find each other without friction.

Our Mission

Empower organisers to reach their audience and help participants discover life-changing opportunities — all for free.

Our Story

EventLink CM was founded in 2026 by a group of young Cameroonians who were tired of missing great events. Whether it was a tech hackathon in Douala, a music night in Yaoundé, or a business forum in Buea — the information was scattered across WhatsApp groups, flyers, and word of mouth.

We built EventLink CM to be the single source of truth for events in Cameroon. Organisers get a powerful, free platform to create and manage events. Participants get beautiful event listings, free digital tickets, and QR-based attendance tracking.

Today, EventLink CM proudly hosts events across Yaoundé, Douala, Buea, Bamenda, and beyond.
