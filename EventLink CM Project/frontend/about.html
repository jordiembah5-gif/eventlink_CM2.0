ABOUT EVENTLINK CM

Bringing Events and People Together

EventLink CM is an event discovery and connection platform designed to make it easier for people to discover, organize and participate in events across Cameroon.

Our Goal

Make event discovery simple and accessible for everyone.

Our Vision

Create a connected, thriving event community in Cameroon.

Our Mission

Help organizers reach attendees and help attendees discover life-changing opportunities.

Our Story

EventLink CM was born out of a simple frustration: missing out on incredible events simply because we didn't know they were happening. We realized that Cameroon has a vibrant culture filled with concerts, tech meetups, business forums, and educational workshops, but there was no central hub to find them.

In 2026, we launched EventLink CM to bridge that gap. Today, we are proud to connect thousands of event-goers with passionate organizers across Douala, Yaoundé, Buea, Bamenda, and beyond.
